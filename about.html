What this is

What's Your Dream is a place to say your dream out loud — as a short video or a few written words — and let people react to it. The crowd votes, comments, and pushes the entries they believe in to the top. The five dreams with the most votes are pinned right on the home page.

Every entry needs an account, so votes and comments stay tied to real people instead of bots refreshing a page.
